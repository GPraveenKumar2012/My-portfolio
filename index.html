Educator. Researcher. Innovator.

I am G. Praveen Kumar, working as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering. My academic interests focus on delivering effective teaching, developing practical programming skills and encouraging students to explore innovative technologies.

My teaching interests include programming, software engineering, web technologies, database systems, cybersecurity and machine learning. I believe in combining theoretical concepts with hands-on implementation and real-world applications.

I am pursuing a part-time Ph.D. at Anna University, registered in January 2024. My research interests include machine learning applications, power quality analysis, predictive modelling and solar energy forecasting.

I am also interested in organizing technical workshops, mentoring students, coordinating academic activities and supporting industry-oriented learning experiences.

My Academic Vision

To create a learning environment that encourages curiosity, critical thinking, technical expertise and research innovation.
